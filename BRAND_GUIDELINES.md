# StudPal Brand Identity & Guidelines

## 1. Colors
* **Primary:** `#112B8A` (Deep Royal Navy)
* **Secondary:** `#8F95A5` (Cool Slate Muted)
* **Accent:** `#2D62FF` (Bright Royal Blue)
* **Background:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
* **Surface:** `#F7F8FA` (Soft Off-White/Neutral Surface)
* **Text:** `#0B0B0F` (Deep Charcoal Text)
* **Link:** `#2D62FF` (Accent Blue)
* **Success:** `#22C55E` (Vibrant Green)
* **Error:** `#EF4444` (Vibrant Red)

---

## 2. Fonts & Typography
* **Font Family:** `Inter` everywhere (Headings, Body, Buttons, Navigation, Forms)
* **Hierarchy:**
  * **H1:** `40px – 56px`, Bold (Weight: 700)
  * **H2:** `24px`, Bold (Weight: 700)
  * **H3:** `20px`, Semibold (Weight: 600)
  * **Body:** `16px`, Regular (Weight: 400)
  * **Small:** `14px`, Regular (Weight: 400)
  * **Caption:** `12px`, Medium (Weight: 500)

---

## 3. Spacing System
* **Base Unit:** `4px`
* Use multiples of 4px consistently (`4px`, `8px`, `12px`, `16px`, `20px`, `24px`, `32px`, `40px`, `48px`, `56px`, `64px`).

---

## 4. Shapes & Border Radius
* **Standard Border Radius:** `12px`
* Use `12px` rounded cards, buttons, inputs, chips, and containers consistently.

---

## 5. Brand Personality
* **Tone:** Modern, friendly, intelligent
* **Energy:** Medium
* **Audience:** Students and Parents
* **StudPal should feel:**
  * Clean
  * Motivating
  * Supportive
  * Smart
  * Slightly playful
  * Premium but approachable

---

## 6. Design Direction
Take inspiration from the strengths of leading study products without copying them:
* **Quizlet:** Simple, approachable study experience
* **Anki:** Powerful spaced repetition
* **Forest:** Motivating study habits
* **Notion:** Flexible organization

**StudPal Formula:**
> **AI + Mastery + Spaced Repetition + Gamification + Flexible Organization + Offline Study**

---

## 7. Core Design Rule
The interface should always make it obvious:
1. **What should I study?**
2. **Why should I study it?**
3. **What should I review next?**
4. **How am I improving?**

*Keep the interface simple enough that a student can understand what to do within a few seconds.*
