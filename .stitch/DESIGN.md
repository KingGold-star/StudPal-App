# StudPal Design System

## 1. Visual Identity & Brand Philosophy
StudPal is an AI-powered study companion and personal learning system. The design feels **clean, motivating, supportive, smart, slightly playful, and premium but approachable** (inspired by the engagement of Forest, educational simplicity of Quizlet, spaced repetition of Anki, and flexibility of Notion).

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#112B8A` (Deep Royal Navy)
- **Brand Accent Orange:** `#FF5B00` (Vibrant Action Orange)
- **Secondary / Muted:** `#8F95A5` (Cool Slate Muted)
- **Accent / Links:** `#2D62FF` (Bright Royal Blue)
- **Surface (Light):** `#F7F8FA`
- **Background (Light):** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text (Light):** `#0B0B0F`
- **Success:** `#22C55E`
- **Error:** `#EF4444`
- **Warning:** `#F59E0B`

## 3. Typography
- **Font Family:** `Inter` (used everywhere for headings, body, buttons, navigation, and inputs)
- **Scale:**
  - H1: 34px–40px, Bold (Weight: 700 / 800)
  - H2: 24px, Bold (Weight: 700)
  - H3: 20px, Semibold (Weight: 600)
  - Body: 15px–16px, Regular (Weight: 400)
  - Small: 14px, Regular (Weight: 400)
  - Caption: 12px, Medium (Weight: 500)

## 4. Layout & Components
- **Base Spacing Unit:** `4px`
- **Border Radius:** `12px` standard for all cards, containers, inputs, and chips. `27px/28px` for full-width primary pill buttons.
- **Buttons:** 54dp–56dp height, high touch target accessibility.

## 5. Design Notes for Stitch Generation
- Device: `MOBILE` (390x844 iPhone / Android viewport)
- Mobile-first clean layout with safe areas.
- High contrast, modern cards, and subtle micro-shadows.
